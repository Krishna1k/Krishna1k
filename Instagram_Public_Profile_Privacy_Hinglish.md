# Instagram Public Profile - Kya Kya Dikhta Hai? (Hinglish Notes)

> 🔴 = Core basics / warning &nbsp;&nbsp; 🟢 = Most important tip

Yeh notes simple Hinglish mein hain. Inhe padh ke aap samajh jaoge ki public account par log (aur tools/websites) aapke baare mein kya kya dekh aur nikaal sakte hain - aur apni privacy kaise bachao.

---

## 0) CORE BASICS - Pehle Yeh Terms Samajh Lo

- 🔴 **Public account** = koi bhi banda (bina aapko follow kiye, bina login kiye bhi) aapki posts, reels, followers sab dekh sakta hai.
- 🔴 **Private account** = sirf approved followers hi aapka content dekh sakte hain. Baaki logon ko sirf DP, naam aur bio dikhta hai.
- 🔴 **Profile / DP** = Display Picture. Public par yeh full size mein download ho sakti hai.
- 🔴 **Bio** = Profile ke neeche ka text. Yahan agar phone/email/link daala ho toh sabko dikhta hai.
- 🔴 **Metadata** = Photo/post ke saath chhupi hui extra info - jaise time, kabhi-kabhi location.
- 🔴 **Scraper / Bot** = Ek automatic program jo aapke public data ko tez tezi se copy (scrape) kar leta hai.
- 🔴 **Web tool / Viewer site** = Websites jo bina login ke public profile ka data dikhane ka dawa karti hain. Yeh sirf public info hi nikaal sakti hain.
- 🔴 **Engagement** = Aapki posts par likes + comments + shares ka total.
- 🔴 **Geotag / Location tag** = Post par lagaya gaya jagah ka naam. Isse location guess hoti hai.
- 🔴 **Tagged photos** = Doosron ki woh photos jismein aapko @ karke tag kiya gaya hai.

---

## 1) Jo HAR KOI Bina Login Ke Dekh Sakta Hai

- Profile photo (DP) - full resolution tak.
- Username, naam aur poora bio (links/email/phone jo bhi daala ho).
- Followers aur Following ka number + poori list.
- Saari posts, reels, photos aur videos - captions aur hashtags ke saath.
- Har post par likes aur comments (aur kisne comment kiya).
- Tagged photos - jin posts mein aap tag ho.
- Story Highlights - jo aapne save karke profile par rakhe hain.
- Location tags - agar aapne post par jagah daali hai.

> 🟢 Yaad rakho: Public matlab "internet par sabke liye khula". Ek baar kuch public ho gaya, log use download/screenshot kar sakte hain - delete karne par bhi unke paas reh sakta hai.

---

## 2) Tools / Scraper Websites EXTRA Kya Nikaalte Hain

- Posting schedule - aap kis time aur din sabse active rehte ho.
- Engagement analytics - real vs fake followers ka andaza.
- Saari images/reels ka bulk download ek click mein.
- Bio ka email/phone uthaa kar list bana lena (spam ke liye).
- Followers/following ka history - kab kisko follow ya unfollow kiya.
- Hashtag aur location se aapki posts ko dhoondh nikaalna.

> 🔴 **Warning:** Bahut saari "free Insta followers / analytics / story viewer" apps aapse login/password maangti hain. Yeh sabse bada khatra hai - account hack ya ban ho sakta hai. **Inhe kabhi password mat do.**

---

## 3) Jo Public Hone Par Bhi Log NAHI Dekh Sakte (bina hacking)

- 🔴 Aapki private DM / messages.
- 🔴 Exact GPS location (jab tak aap khud share na karo).
- 🔴 Phone number ya email - agar woh bio/contact mein hide hai.
- 🔴 Kisne aapki story dekhi - yeh sirf aapko dikhta hai.
- 🔴 "Close Friends" wali stories - sirf chuni hui list ko.
- 🔴 Archived (chhupayi hui) posts aur draft.

> 🟢 Matlab: Sabse zyada control aapke apne haath mein hai - aap kya post karte ho aur settings kaise rakhte ho, wahi tay karta hai ki log kya dekhenge.

---

## 4) Privacy Bachane Ke Top Tips (Agar Public Rakhna Hai)

1. 🟢 Bio se phone number aur personal email hata do. Zaroori ho toh business/throwaway email use karo.
2. 🟢 Location tags **OFF** rakho - khaaskar ghar, school ya daily jaane wali jagah ke.
3. 🟢 Sensitive cheezein "Close Friends" story mein daalo, public story mein nahi.
4. 🟢 Kisi bhi third-party "followers/analytics/viewer" app ko apna password kabhi mat do.
5. 🟢 Settings mein **2-Factor Authentication (2FA)** ON karo - account chori se bachta hai.
6. Settings > "Show Activity Status" OFF karo (online kab the, chhupega).
7. "Allow Sharing of your story" jaise options soch samajh kar rakho.
8. Kisi anjaan link par tap karke kabhi login mat karo (phishing se bacho).

---

## 5) Quick Self-Check (Public Karne Se Pehle)

Apne aap se poocho:

- Kya meri DP/posts mein ghar ka address ya phone number dikh raha hai?
- Kya kisi photo mein school ID, gaadi ka number, ya documents hain?
- Kya location tags se koi mera daily routine guess kar sakta hai?
- Kya bio mein aisi info hai jo galat haathon mein nuksaan kar sakti hai?

> 🟢 Agar in mein se kisi ka jawab "haan" hai - toh ya woh content hata do, ya account private rakho.

---

## Ek Line Mein Nichod

- 🔴 Public profile = aapki har public cheez sabke liye khuli aur copy-able hai.
- 🟢 Smart raho: kam personal info daalo, location off rakho, password kisi app ko mat do, aur 2FA on rakho. Bas itna karoge toh public rehkar bhi kaafi safe rahoge.
