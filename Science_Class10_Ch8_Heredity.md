## Core Basics - Pehle Ye Padho (Class 7-8-9 Ka Foundation)

🔴 Tune 7-8-9 nahi padhi - koi tension nahi. Ye page is chapter ki base hai.
🔴 Ek baar achhe se padh le, fir poora chapter aasaani se samajh aayega.

🔴 1) CELL aur NUCLEUS (Class 8 ka idea):
🔴    - Cell = jeev ki sabse chhoti unit (body ki "eent"/brick).
🔴    - Nucleus = cell ka "control room" (beech me). Isi me sari genetic
🔴      information (DNA) rehti hai jo trait decide karti hai.

🔴 2) DNA, CHROMOSOME, GENE (sabse zaroori - intro):
🔴    - DNA = ek lamba thread-jaisa molecule jisme "instructions" likhe hote.
🔴    - CHROMOSOME = DNA jab tightly lipti/packed hoti to thread-jaisi structure
🔴      banti = chromosome. Nucleus me jodi (pairs) me hote hain.
🔴    - GENE = DNA/chromosome ka ek chhota tukda jo EK trait control karta
🔴      (jaise height ka gene, seed colour ka gene).

🔴 3) GAMETE (sex cell):
🔴    - Reproduction ke liye banne wali special cell = gamete.
🔴    - Male gamete = sperm (shukraanu), Female gamete = egg/ovum (andaanu).
🔴    - Gamete me normal cell se AADHE (half) chromosome hote hain.

🔴 4) FERTILISATION aur ZYGOTE:
🔴    - Sperm + egg ka milna = FERTILISATION.
🔴    - Milne ke baad bani pehli single cell = ZYGOTE. Yahi badh ke baby banti.
🔴    - Zygote me dono parents ke chromosome aa jaate (aadhe maa + aadhe baap se).

🔴 5) TRAIT / CHARACTER kya hai:
🔴    - Trait = jeev ka koi feature/lakshan (height, eye colour, seed shape).
🔴    - Ye traits genes ke through parents se bacchon me aate hain.

🔴 6) DOMINANT vs RECESSIVE (simple intro):
🔴    - DOMINANT = jo trait dikh jaata hai (express ho jaata), bhale doosra bhi ho.
🔴    - RECESSIVE = jo dab jaata hai, dikhta tabhi jab dono allele recessive ho.

🔴 7) VARIATION:
🔴    - Ek hi species ke jeevon me chhote-chhote farak (differences) = variation.
🔴    - Reproduction (khaas kar sexual) se variation aata hai - sab bilkul same
🔴      nahi hote.

🔴 8) SEX CHROMOSOMES (XX / XY) ka idea:
🔴    - Kuch chromosome jeev ka GENDER decide karte = sex chromosomes.
🔴    - Insaan me: female = XX, male = XY. (Detail aage main content me.)

🔴 9) MITOSIS vs MEIOSIS (simple idea):
🔴    - MITOSIS = normal body cell division; chromosome number SAME rehta
🔴      (growth/repair me kaam aata).
🔴    - MEIOSIS = gamete banane wali division; chromosome number AADHA ho jaata
🔴      (isiliye gamete me half chromosome hote hain).

1. HEREDITY aur INHERITED TRAITS
🟢 HEREDITY = parents se bacchon me traits (lakshan) ka transfer hona.
   - Inheritance/Anuvanshikta wahi process hai.

   Bachhe apne parents jaise kyun dikhte hain?
🟢  - Kyunki parents apne GENES (DNA) gametes (sperm/egg) ke through bacche ko
🟢    dete hain. Zygote me dono parents ke gene aate, isliye bacche me dono ki
🟢    jhalak dikhti.
   - Har trait ke liye bacche ko 2 copy (allele) milte: 1 maa se, 1 baap se.

   🔴 INHERITED TRAIT = jo gene ke through mile (height, eye colour, blood group).
   🔴 Ye hi traits aage ki generation me jaate hain.

2. MENDEL aur unke MATAR (PEA PLANT) EXPERIMENTS
   Gregor Mendel ko "Father of Genetics" kehte hain. Unhone garden pea plant
   (scientific naam: Pisum sativum) par cross karke inheritance ke rules diye.


## 2.1 Mendel Ne Pea Plant Hi Kyun Chuna

---------------------------------------------------------
🟢 Ye exam me favourite question hai - reasons yaad rakho:
🟢   (i)   Pea me CLEAR contrasting traits the (tall/short, round/wrinkled seed,
🟢         green/yellow seed) - aasaani se pehchaan aate.
🟢   (ii)  Cross karna (cross-pollination) aasaan + self-pollination bhi ho sakti,
🟢         to Mendel control kar sakte the.
🟢   (iii) Life cycle CHHOTA tha aur ek baar me bahut saare seeds milte the
🟢         (zyada data + jaldi results).


## 2.2 Kuch Zaroori Terms (Definitions)

---------------------------------------------------------
🟢 GENE = DNA ka tukda jo ek trait control karta.
🟢 ALLELE = ek hi gene ke alag-alag roop (jaise T = tall allele, t = short allele).
🟢 GENOTYPE = jeev ka genetic make-up (allele ka combination), jaise TT, Tt, tt.
🟢 PHENOTYPE = jo bahar DIKHTA hai (physical roop), jaise "tall" ya "short".
🟢 DOMINANT = jo allele express ho jaata (capital letter, e.g. T).
🟢 RECESSIVE = jo dab jaata, sirf double recessive me dikhta (small letter, e.g. t).
🟢 HOMOZYGOUS = dono allele SAME (TT ya tt) - ise "pure" bhi kehte.
🟢 HETEROZYGOUS = dono allele ALAG (Tt) - ise "hybrid" bhi kehte.
   F1 GENERATION = parents (P) ke cross se bani pehli santaan (first filial).
   F2 GENERATION = F1 ko aapas me cross/self karne se bani agli santaan.

## 3. Monohybrid Cross (Ek Trait)

🟢 MONOHYBRID = ek hi trait (jaise height) ko dekhte hue cross. Bahut important.

   Cross: pure Tall (TT)  x  pure short (tt)
   - Tall ke gamete = T, short ke gamete = t.
   - F1 me sab milte = Tt  -> SAARE TALL (kyunki T dominant, t recessive).
🟢  - Matlab F1 me short trait GAYAB ho gaya (par gene chhupa hua hai).

   Ab F1 ko self/cross karo: Tt  x  Tt
   - Gametes: T aur t (dono parents se).

   PUNNETT SQUARE (F1 x F1):

            |   T    |   t
       -----+--------+--------
         T  |   TT   |   Tt
       -----+--------+--------
         t  |   Tt   |   tt

🟢 F2 RESULTS (RATTA maaro):
🟢   - PHENOTYPE ratio = 3 Tall : 1 short = 3:1
🟢   - GENOTYPE ratio  = 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt = 1:2:1
🟢  - Short trait (tt) F2 me WAPAS aa gaya -> saabit hua gene chhupa tha (recessive).

   🔴 Note: 3 tall me se 1 pure (TT) aur 2 hybrid (Tt) hote hain.

## 4. Dihybrid Cross (Do Traits)

🟢 DIHYBRID = ek saath DO traits dekhte hue cross. Ratio 9:3:3:1 important hai.

   Cross: Round-Yellow seed (RRYY)  x  wrinkled-green seed (rryy)
   - F1 sab = RrYy -> sab Round aur Yellow (R aur Y dominant hain).

   Ab F1 self karo: RrYy x RrYy
   - Har parent ke 4 type ke gamete: RY, Ry, rY, ry.

   PUNNETT SQUARE (16 boxes):

           |  RY   |  Ry   |  rY   |  ry
      -----+-------+-------+-------+-------
       RY  | RRYY  | RRYy  | RrYY  | RrYy
      -----+-------+-------+-------+-------
       Ry  | RRYy  | RRyy  | RrYy  | Rryy
      -----+-------+-------+-------+-------
       rY  | RrYY  | RrYy  | rrYY  | rrYy
      -----+-------+-------+-------+-------
       ry  | RrYy  | Rryy  | rrYy  | rryy

🟢 F2 PHENOTYPE ratio = 9 : 3 : 3 : 1
🟢   - 9 Round-Yellow : 3 Round-green : 3 wrinkled-Yellow : 1 wrinkled-green.
🟢 CONCLUSION: do traits ALAG-ALAG (INDEPENDENTLY) inherit hote hain - ek trait
🟢 doosre ko affect nahi karta. Isi se naye combos (Round-green, wrinkled-Yellow)
🟢 bante jo parents me alag the.

5. INHERITANCE KE RULES aur TRAIT KAISE EXPRESS HOTA

5.1 RULES OF INHERITANCE (Mendel se)
---------------------------------------------------------
🟢  - Traits genes ke through control hote hain.
🟢  - Har trait ke 2 allele hote; har PARENT ek allele deta (gamete ke through).
🟢  - Heterozygous (Tt) me DOMINANT allele express hota, recessive dab jaata.
   - Gamete banne par allele alag ho jaate (ek hi allele gamete me jaata).

5.2 TRAIT KAISE EXPRESS HOTA HAI (GENE -> PROTEIN -> TRAIT)
---------------------------------------------------------
   - Ek gene asal me ek PROTEIN (ya enzyme) banane ka code rakhta hai.
   - Wahi protein/enzyme body me kaam karke trait banata hai.
   Example (simple): plant me "tallness" ka ek gene enzyme banata jo hormone
   zyada banata -> plant tall. Agar allele aisa ho jo enzyme kam-asar banaye
   to plant short reh jaata.
🟢  - Short me: GENE -> PROTEIN/ENZYME -> TRAIT. Dominant allele wala efficient
🟢    protein banata, isliye uska trait dikh jaata.

## 6. Sex Determination In Humans (Ladka Ya Ladki)

🟢 Insaan me 23 PAIR (yaani 46) chromosomes hote hain.
🟢   - In me se 22 pair = autosomes (body wale).
🟢   - 1 pair = SEX CHROMOSOMES (gender decide karte).
🟢 FEMALE = XX  ,  MALE = XY.

   Gametes me kya jaata hai?
🟢  - Maa (XX) ke saare eggs me sirf X aata (mother HAMESHA X deti).
🟢  - Baap (XY) ke sperm me ya to X ya Y aata.

   PUNNETT SQUARE (Mother XX  x  Father XY):

           |   X    |   X
      -----+--------+--------
       X   |   XX   |   XX
      -----+--------+--------
       Y   |   XY   |   XY

🟢 RESULT: agar baap ka X wala sperm jeeta -> XX -> LADKI.
🟢         agar baap ka Y wala sperm jeeta -> XY -> LADKA.
🟢 Matlab BACCHE KA GENDER BAAP KE SPERM se decide hota (50-50 chance).
   🔴 Isiliye ladki ke liye maa ko blame karna galat aur unscientific hai.

7. ACQUIRED vs INHERITED TRAITS
🟢 Ye difference exam me bahut poocha jaata - dono ko alag samjho.

   INHERITED (genetic) TRAITS:
🟢  - Gene/DNA ke through parents se mile (eye colour, blood group, height).
🟢  - Ye AGLI generation me jaate hain (kyunki gametes/DNA me hote hain).


## Acquired Traits:

🟢  - Jeevan ke dauraan body par aaye changes (kam weight starvation se,
🟢    kati hui tail/dumka, body building se bani muscles, seekhi hui language).
🟢  - Ye AGLI generation me NAHI jaate.

🟢 KYUN NAHI JAATE (reason - yaad rakho):
🟢  - Acquired changes sirf BODY (somatic) cells ko affect karte hain, ye
🟢    germ/reproductive cells ke DNA ko change NAHI karte.
🟢  - Gamete ka DNA hi agli pidhi me jaata - jab wahi nahi badla, to acquired
🟢    trait pass nahi hota.
   🔴 Classic example: agar chuhe (mouse) ki tail kaat do, to bhi uske bacche
   🔴 poori tail ke saath hi paida honge (kaatna DNA nahi badalta).

8. SOLVED EXAMPLES (HARDEST -> EASIEST)
🟢 Ye type ke questions exam me pakka aate - steps khud likhke practice karo.

EXAMPLE 1 (Hardest): Dihybrid cross karke dikhao ki F2 me 9:3:3:1 kaise aata
   aur isse kya conclusion nikalta?
   - Parents: RRYY (round-yellow) x rryy (wrinkled-green).
   - F1: sab RrYy (round-yellow) kyunki R aur Y dominant.
   - F1 x F1 -> 4 gametes (RY, Ry, rY, ry) -> 16 box wala Punnett square.
   - F2 phenotype: 9 round-yellow, 3 round-green, 3 wrinkled-yellow,
     1 wrinkled-green = 9:3:3:1.
   - Conclusion: do traits INDEPENDENTLY inherit hote (ek doosre ko affect nahi).

EXAMPLE 2: Tall (TT) x short (tt) ka monohybrid cross Punnett square se dikhao;
   F2 ka phenotype aur genotype ratio batao.
   - F1: sab Tt (tall). F1 self -> Tt x Tt.
        |  T   |  t
     ---+------+------
      T |  TT  |  Tt
     ---+------+------
      t |  Tt  |  tt
   - Phenotype = 3 tall : 1 short = 3:1.
   - Genotype  = 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt = 1:2:1.

EXAMPLE 3: Cross Tt x tt diya hai. Offspring ka genotype aur phenotype ratio?
        |  T   |  t
     ---+------+------
      t |  Tt  |  tt
     ---+------+------
      t |  Tt  |  tt
   - Genotype = 2 Tt : 2 tt = 1:1.  Phenotype = 1 tall : 1 short = 1:1.

EXAMPLE 4: Cross dikha ke explain karo bacche ka sex (gender) baap se kaise tay
   hota hai.
   - Mother XX (sirf X deti), Father XY (X ya Y deta).
        |  X   |  X
     ---+------+------
      X |  XX  |  XX
      Y |  XY  |  XY
   - XX = ladki, XY = ladka. Y baap ke sperm se aata, isliye gender BAAP decide
     karta (50-50 chance).

EXAMPLE 5: Tall x short cross me F1 sab tall the, aur F2 me 3 tall : 1 short
   mile. Konsa trait dominant aur konsa recessive? Kaise pata chala?
   - Dominant = TALL (F1 me sirf yahi dikha).
   - Recessive = SHORT (F1 me chhup gaya, F2 me wapas 1/4 me aaya).
   - Jo F1 me dikhe woh dominant; jo chhup ke F2 me laute woh recessive.

EXAMPLE 6: Ek aadmi ne body-building se strong muscles banaye. Kya ye trait
   uske bacchon me jaayega? Kyun?
   - NAHI jaayega. Ye ACQUIRED trait hai - sirf body (somatic) cells par asar,
     germ cells/gamete ke DNA me koi change nahi. Isliye inherit nahi hota.

EXAMPLE 7 (Easiest): Mendel ne apne experiment ke liye pea plant hi kyun chuna?
   - Clear contrasting traits (tall/short, round/wrinkled), cross/self dono
     aasaan, aur chhota life cycle + bahut saare seeds (jaldi + zyada data).

## 9. Quick Q&A - Padhne Ke Baad Khud Check Karo

🟢 Pehle khud answer socho, fir niche milao. Ye board-style questions hain.

Q1. Heredity kise kehte hain?
A1. Parents se santaan me traits (lakshan) ka genes ke through transfer hona.

Q2. Genotype aur phenotype me farak?
A2. Genotype = andar ka genetic make-up (TT/Tt/tt). Phenotype = bahar dikhne
    wala roop (tall/short).

Q3. Homozygous aur heterozygous me farak?
A3. Homozygous = dono allele same (TT ya tt). Heterozygous = dono allele alag (Tt).

Q4. Monohybrid cross ka F2 phenotype aur genotype ratio?
A4. Phenotype 3:1 (tall:short); genotype 1:2:1 (TT:Tt:tt).

Q5. Dihybrid cross ka F2 phenotype ratio aur conclusion?
A5. 9:3:3:1; conclusion - do traits independently inherit hote hain.

Q6. Insaan me ladki paida hone ke liye baap ne konsa chromosome diya?
A6. X chromosome (X + X = XX = ladki). Y se ladka banta.

Q7. Bacche ka gender kaun decide karta hai aur kyun?
A7. Baap (father), kyunki maa hamesha X deti par baap X ya Y - jo sperm jeete
    wahi gender tay karta.

Q8. Acquired trait kya hai? Ek example.
A8. Jeevan me body par aaya change (jaise kati hui tail / banayi hui muscles).
    Ye agli generation me nahi jaata.

Q9. Acquired traits agli pidhi me kyun nahi jaate?
A9. Kyunki ye germ/reproductive cells ke DNA ko change nahi karte; sirf gamete
    ka DNA aage jaata hai.

Q10. Dominant aur recessive allele me farak?
A10. Dominant express ho jaata hai (Tt me bhi dikhta); recessive tabhi dikhta
     jab dono allele recessive hon (tt).
