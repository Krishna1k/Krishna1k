# 🔴 PART A — CORE BASICS (MISS MAT KARNA)

### 🔴 1. Cell, Nucleus, DNA, Chromosome, Gene
- 🔴 Nucleus me **chromosome** (DNA+protein). **Gene** = DNA ka tukda jo 1 trait control kare. Human me **23 jodi (46)** chromosome.

### 🔴 2. Trait, Gamete, Fertilisation
- 🔴 **Trait** = lakshan (height/rang). **Gamete** me aadhe (23). Fertilisation: sperm(23)+egg(23)→zygote(46).

### 🔴 3. Heredity + Variation
- 🔴 **Heredity** = parents→bachche traits transfer. **Variation** = antar. Bachcha parents jaisa par same nahi.

### 🔴 4. Dominant/Recessive
- 🔴 Har trait ke 2 gene copy. Jo dikhta = **Dominant**, jo chhupta = **Recessive**.

### 🔴 5. Probability
- 🔴 Hone ki sambhavna (0-1 / %). Sikka → head/tail 1/2. Genetics ratio isi se.

# PART B — CHAPTER KA MAIN CONTENT

### 1. 🟢 Heredity aur Mendel
- 🟢 **Mendel** ("Father of Genetics") ne **matar (pea, Pisum sativum)** par kaam kiya. Kyun? Jaldi badhta, clear traits, self+cross pollination.

### 2. 🟢 Key Terms — VERY IMPORTANT (ratta)
| Term | Matlab |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Gene | trait wala DNA tukda |
| 🟢 Allele | gene ke roop (T=lamba, t=bauna) |
| 🟢 Dominant | dikhta (T) |
| 🟢 Recessive | dabba (t) |
| 🟢 Genotype | gene combo (TT, Tt, tt) |
| 🟢 Phenotype | jo dikhta (lamba/bauna) |
| 🟢 Homozygous | same allele (TT/tt) |
| 🟢 Heterozygous | alag allele (Tt) |

### 3. 🟢 Monohybrid Cross — TOP EXAM AREA
- 🟢 P: **TT × tt** → F1: sab **Tt** (saare lambe).
- 🟢 F1×F1 (Tt×Tt) → F2 genotype **1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt**, phenotype **3 lamba : 1 bauna (3:1)**.
- 🟢 Recessive (bauna) F1 me gayab → F2 me wapas → gene jodon me hote, segregate karte.

### 4. 🟢 Dihybrid Cross — VERY IMPORTANT
- 🟢 P: **RRYY × rryy** → F1: sab RrYy. **F2 phenotype = 9:3:3:1**.
- 🟢 Naye combinations (round-green, wrinkled-yellow) → traits **swatantra (independent)** inherit hote.

### 5. 🟢 Mendel ke Niyam
- 🟢 **(1) Dominance** (ek hi dikhta), **(2) Segregation** (gamete me ek allele), **(3) Independent Assortment** (traits swatantra).

### 6. 🟢 Sex Determination in Humans — TOP EXAM AREA
- 🟢 22 jodi **autosomes** + 1 jodi **sex chromosome**. Female **XX**, Male **XY**.
- 🟢 Mother hamesha X; Father X ya Y. Father X → XX (ladki); Father Y → XY (ladka).
- 🟢 **Bachche ka gender FATHER decide karta** (mother nahi) — exam favourite.

### 7. 🟢 Acquired vs Inherited
- 🟢 **Inherited** (gene se, agli gen me jaata — aankh rang). **Acquired** (jeevan me khud — gym muscle, kata haath) → DNA me nahi → agli gen me **nahi** jaata.

# PART C — SOLVED EXAMPLES (HARDEST → EASIEST)

### ⭐ Example 1 (HARDEST) — Monohybrid cross
TT×tt → F1 Tt (lambe). Tt×Tt → F2 **1TT:2Tt:1tt**, phenotype **3:1**. Recessive tt me wapas dikha → gene pairs me, segregate.

### Example 2 — Dihybrid F2
RRYY×rryy → F1 RrYy → F2 **9:3:3:1**. Naye combinations → Law of Independent Assortment.

### Example 3 — Sex determination
Mother X, Father X/Y. X→ladki, Y→ladka. **Father** decide karta.

### Example 4 — Acquired vs inherited
Gym muscle = acquired (gene me nahi) → bachche me nahi aayega.

### Example 5 (EASIEST) — Genotype vs Phenotype
Genotype = gene combo (TT/Tt/tt); Phenotype = dikhne wala (lamba/bauna).
