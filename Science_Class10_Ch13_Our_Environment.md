# 🔴 PART A — CORE BASICS (MISS MAT KARNA)

### 🔴 1. Environment aur Organisms
- 🔴 **Biotic** = jeevit (paudhe, jaanwar, bacteria); **Abiotic** = nirjeev (hawa, paani, dhoop, mitti).

### 🔴 2. Nutrition types
- 🔴 **Autotroph/Producer** (green plants), **Heterotroph/Consumer** (jaanwar), **Decomposer** (bacteria, fungi).

### 🔴 3. Energy aur Food
- 🔴 Energy ka source = **Suraj**. Producer suraj→food. **Food chain** = kaun kisko khata.

### 🔴 4. Photosynthesis + Respiration
- 🔴 Photosynthesis (CO₂+paani+sunlight→glucose+O₂); Respiration (glucose→energy). Energy kabhi 100% transfer nahi.

### 🔴 5. Pollution
- 🔴 Harmful cheezein environment me. Kuch sad jaati (natural), kuch nahi (plastic).

# PART B — CHAPTER KA MAIN CONTENT

### 1. 🟢 Ecosystem
- 🟢 Biotic + abiotic milkar interact. Natural (forest, pond), artificial (garden, aquarium).

### 2. 🟢 Producers, Consumers, Decomposers — VERY IMPORTANT
- 🟢 **Producers** (plants, photosynthesis). **Consumers:** herbivore/carnivore/omnivore/parasite.
- 🟢 **Decomposers** (bacteria, fungi) — nutrients wapas environment me (**recycling**). Inke bina nutrients atak jaate.

### 3. 🟢 Food Chain aur Food Web — TOP EXAM AREA
- 🟢 **Food chain:** Grass→Deer→Lion. **Trophic levels:** T1 producer, T2 herbivore, T3/T4 carnivore.
- 🟢 **Food web** = bahut food chains juda (real nature).

### 4. 🟢 Energy Flow + 10% Law — TOP EXAM (VERY IMP)
- 🟢 Energy **unidirectional** (Sun→Producer→Consumer, wapas nahi).
- 🟢 **10% Law:** ek level se agle me sirf ~10% energy; 90% heat/respiration me. Isliye food chain **3-4 steps**. Energy pyramid **upright**.

### 5. 🟢 Biomagnification
- 🟢 Harmful chemicals (DDT/pesticide) food chain me upar **concentrate** hote → **top consumer** (insaan) me sabse jyada.

### 6. 🟢 Biodegradable vs Non-biodegradable — TOP EXAM AREA
- 🟢 **Biodegradable** (decomposers se sad jaate): sabzi-chhilke, kaagaz, lakdi, cow dung, cotton.
- 🟢 **Non-biodegradable** (nahi sadte): plastic, polythene, glass, metal, DDT.
- 🟢 Problem: non-biodeg → pollution, naali jaam, animals nigal lete.

### 7. 🟢 Ozone Layer + Depletion — VERY IMPORTANT
- 🟢 **Ozone (O₃)** stratosphere me; harmful **UV** kirno ko rokta (skin cancer/cataract se bachav).
- 🟢 Banta: `O₂ --UV--> O+O`, `O+O₂→O₃`. **Depletion:** **CFCs** (fridge/AC/spray) ozone todte.
- 🟢 **Montreal Protocol (1987):** CFC kam/band karne ka global agreement.

### 8. 🟢 Waste Management
- 🟢 Segregation, recycling, composting, sewage treatment, landfill, incineration.
- 🟢 **3 R:** Reduce, Reuse, Recycle. Hara bin (wet/biodeg) + neela bin (dry/non-biodeg).
