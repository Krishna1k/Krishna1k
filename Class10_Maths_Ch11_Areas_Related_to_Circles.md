## SECTION 1: Foundation

### 1.1 Circle ke basic formulas (yaad rakh)

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Circumference (perimeter)** | `2πr` (or `πd`) |
| **Area of circle** | `πr²` |
| **Diameter** | `d = 2r` |

Yahan:
- `r` = radius
- `π (pi)` ≈ **22/7** (ya 3.14)

### 1.2 π (pi) kya hai?

π = circumference / diameter ka ratio. Har circle ke liye **same** hota hai ≈ 3.14159...

Exam mein:
- Agar radius **7 ka multiple** ho → use **π = 22/7**
- Warna → use **π = 3.14**

### 1.3 Example: basic

#### Example 1: Radius 7 cm wale circle ka area aur circumference

```
Area = πr² = (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 22 × 7 = 154 cm²
Circumference = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 7 = 44 cm
```

## SECTION 2: Sector of a Circle

### 2.1 Sector kya hai?

**Sector** = circle ka ek "pizza slice" — do radii aur ek arc ke beech ka area.

Angle θ (theta) us slice ka angle hai (centre pe).

```
         arc
        ____
      /      \
     / sector \
    /    θ     \
   *-----O-----*
   radius  radius
```

### 2.2 Formulas (θ degrees mein)

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Area of sector** | `(θ/360) × πr²` |
| **Length of arc** | `(θ/360) × 2πr` |
| **Perimeter of sector** | `arc length + 2r` |

**Logic:** Pura circle 360° hai. Sector us ka `θ/360` hissa hai. So area aur arc bhi utna hi fraction.

### 2.3 Examples

#### Example 2: Radius 21 cm, angle 60°. Sector ka area nikaal.

```
Area = (θ/360) × πr²
     = (60/360) × (22/7) × 21 × 21
     = (1/6) × (22/7) × 441
     = (1/6) × 22 × 63
     = (1/6) × 1386
     = 231 cm²
```

**Sector area = 231 cm²** ✅

#### Example 3: Radius 7 cm, angle 90°. Arc length nikaal.

```
Arc = (θ/360) × 2πr
    = (90/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 7
    = (1/4) × 44
    = 11 cm
```

**Arc length = 11 cm** ✅

## SECTION 3: Segment of a Circle

### 3.1 Segment kya hai?

**Segment** = chord aur arc ke beech ka area (sector mein se triangle nikaal do).

```
        ____
      /  seg  \
     /._______.\   ← chord
    /  triangle \
   *------O------*
```

### 3.2 Formula

```
Area of (minor) segment = Area of sector - Area of triangle
```

- Area of sector = `(θ/360) × πr²`
- Area of triangle (with two sides = r and angle θ between) = `(1/2) r² sin θ`

So:
```
Segment area = (θ/360) × πr² - (1/2) r² sin θ
```

### 3.3 Example

#### Example 4: Radius 10 cm, angle 90°. Minor segment ka area. (π = 3.14)

```
Sector area = (90/360) × 3.14 × 10 × 10
            = (1/4) × 314 = 78.5 cm²

Triangle area = (1/2) × r² × sin 90°
              = (1/2) × 100 × 1 = 50 cm²

Segment = 78.5 - 50 = 28.5 cm²
```

**Minor segment area = 28.5 cm²** ✅

## SECTION 4: Minor vs Major

- **Minor sector/segment:** chhota wala (angle < 180°)
- **Major sector/segment:** bada wala (angle > 180°)
- Major area = (Total circle area) − (Minor area)
- Major angle = 360° − minor angle

## SUMMARY

1. **Circumference** = `2πr`, **Area** = `πr²`
2. **π** = 22/7 (jab radius 7 ka multiple) ya 3.14
3. **Sector area** = `(θ/360) × πr²`
4. **Arc length** = `(θ/360) × 2πr`
5. **Perimeter of sector** = arc length + 2r
6. **Segment area** = sector area − triangle area = `(θ/360)πr² − (1/2)r² sin θ`
7. **Major** = total − minor
