## SECTION 1: Foundation

### 1.1 Surface Area vs Volume (basic difference)

- **Surface Area (SA):** solid ke bahar ki total area (paint karne layak) — unit **cm²/m²**
- **Volume:** solid ke andar ki jagah (paani bharne layak) — unit **cm³/m³**

Do tarah ki surface area:
- **CSA (Curved/Lateral Surface Area):** sirf curved/side part (top-bottom chhod ke)
- **TSA (Total Surface Area):** sab kuch (curved + flat faces)

### 1.2 π reminder

`π = 22/7` (jab radius 7 ka multiple) ya `3.14`.

## SECTION 2: Basic Solids ke Formulas (Yaad rakh — sabse important)

### 2.1 Cube (sides = a)
| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| TSA | `6a²` |
| Volume | `a³` |

### 2.2 Cuboid (length l, breadth b, height h)
| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| TSA | `2(lb + bh + hl)` |
| Volume | `l × b × h` |

### 2.3 Cylinder (radius r, height h)
| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| CSA | `2πrh` |
| TSA | `2πr(r + h)` |
| Volume | `πr²h` |

### 2.4 Cone (radius r, height h, slant height l)
| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Slant height | `l = √(r² + h²)` |
| CSA | `πrl` |
| TSA | `πr(l + r)` |
| Volume | `(1/3)πr²h` |

### 2.5 Sphere (radius r)
| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Surface Area | `4πr²` |
| Volume | `(4/3)πr³` |

### 2.6 Hemisphere (radius r) — aadha sphere
| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| CSA | `2πr²` |
| TSA | `3πr²` (curved + flat circle) |
| Volume | `(2/3)πr³` |

## SECTION 3: Examples (single solids)

#### Example 1: Cylinder radius 7 cm, height 10 cm. Volume aur CSA. (π = 22/7)

```
Volume = πr²h = (22/7) × 7 × 7 × 10 = 22 × 7 × 10 = 1540 cm³
CSA = 2πrh = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 10 = 440 cm²
```

#### Example 2: Cone radius 6 cm, height 8 cm. Slant height aur volume nikaal.

```
l = √(r² + h²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 cm
Volume = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3) × 3.14 × 36 × 8 = 301.44 cm³
```

#### Example 3: Sphere radius 7 cm. Surface area. (π = 22/7)

```
SA = 4πr² = 4 × (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 4 × 22 × 7 = 616 cm²
```

## SECTION 4: Combination of Solids (Asli Chapter 10 ka focus)

Class 10 mein zyadatar solids **jodke** (combine) banaye jaate hain — jaise ice-cream cone (cone + hemisphere), capsule (cylinder + 2 hemispheres), etc.

### 4.1 Golden Rule

> Jab solids jodte hain:
> - **Surface area ADD karo sirf EXPOSED (dikhne wale) surfaces ka** (jo surface chhup gaya use mat ginna)
> - **Volume hamesha simply ADD karo**

### 4.2 Example 4: Ice-cream (cone + hemisphere on top)

Cone (radius 3.5 cm, height 12 cm) ke upar hemisphere (radius 3.5 cm). Total volume nikaal. (π = 22/7)

```
Cone volume = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3) × (22/7) × 3.5 × 3.5 × 12
            = (1/3) × (22/7) × 12.25 × 12
            = 154 cm³

Hemisphere volume = (2/3)πr³ = (2/3) × (22/7) × 3.5³
                  = (2/3) × (22/7) × 42.875
                  = 89.83 cm³ (approx)

Total = 154 + 89.83 ≈ 243.83 cm³
```

### 4.3 Surface area example logic (capsule)

Capsule = cylinder + 2 hemispheres (ends).
- Total SA = (CSA of cylinder) + (2 × CSA of hemisphere)
- = `2πrh + 2 × 2πr²`
- Cylinder ke flat circular ends count NAHI hote (hemisphere se dhak gaye).

## SECTION 5: Frustum of a Cone (Optional / Advanced)

**Frustum** = cone ka upar wala hissa kaat do, jo neeche bachta hai (jaise bucket, glass).

Radii R (bada, neeche) aur r (chhota, upar), height h, slant height l:

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Slant height | `l = √(h² + (R-r)²)` |
| CSA | `π(R + r)l` |
| TSA | `π(R + r)l + πR² + πr²` |
| Volume | `(1/3)πh(R² + r² + Rr)` |
